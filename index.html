About Me

Arman, better known as his artist persona 'Bread', is an artist, designer, and leader in the creative field with over five years of professional experience in multiple creative and corporate industries. He is currently the Chief Creative Officer for Uy Records and Entertainment in charge of marketing and creative teams within the music and entertainment industry.

Bread believes that creativity is the application of artistic skill and knowledge interwoven with ones personal experiences and unique taste. This philosophy makes him highly adaptable in bringing creativity to various industries while staying true to his flair.

Outside corporate work, Bread writes lore and fictional stories to pass time, and creates electronic music when he feels like it. He also supports non-profit interest groups by providing creative services and mentoring budding creatives.
